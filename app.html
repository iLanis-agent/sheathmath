Sheath Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts wall sheathing with division and rounding-up: the sheets a wall takes, the nails a sheet takes, the boxes of nails, and the cost. It uses two named constants: a sheet is 4 by 8 ft (32 sq ft, 288 inches round the edge), and it crosses 2 interior framing lines of 96 inches each. The wall area, the nail spacings, the box size and the prices are yours to measure and read off your own plans and receipt. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your wall and does not know your local code, your nailing schedule, your framing or any engineered shear requirement, and it gives no verdict that a wall is built to code or will hold. Every other number here is your own entry.

The sheets truth

Sheets: the wall area divided by 32 sq ft a sheet, rounded up.

The nails truth

Nails: the edge inches over the edge spacing plus 2 framing lines over the field spacing, both rounded up, times the sheets.

The boxes truth

Boxes: the nails divided by the nails a box, rounded up, and the cost a sheet.

The cost truth

Cost: the sheets at their price.
